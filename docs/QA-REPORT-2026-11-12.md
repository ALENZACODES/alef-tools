## Found on the way

1. **The live site sends no Content-Security-Policy header.** `curl -I https://alef-review.onrender.com/` on 13 November 2026 01:47 UTC
   returned `x-content-type-options: nosniff` and no CSP. `render.yaml` carries the policy, but its own header says the live
   service is not managed from that file. Until the headers are set on the Render service, "no request goes anywhere else"
   rests on the code and its tests, not on the browser.
2. **Al-Heb `main` has a test that fails on Fridays (UTC).** `tests/shell.rimon-companion.test.js`, "one greeting line a day",
   fails whenever the clock says Friday; CI runs in UTC, and it failed on `main` at 00:14 UTC on 13 November. It is not touched by
   this lane.
3. **Pack host size.** The packs planned in PLAN.md add up to about 2.2 GiB. GitHub Pages recommends keeping a site under 1 GB.
   The pack host will need a second Pages repository (same `alenzacodes.github.io` origin, so no CSP change) or another static host
   before all packs are published.
4. **`onnx-community/whisper-small` and `ivrit-ai/whisper-large-v3-turbo-onnx` carry no licence tag on the Hub.**
   Both are ports of Apache-2.0 models and are recorded that way, with the note. John may want to confirm before either ships.
5. **PLAN.md's "danielrosehill/Parakeet-TDT-0.6B EN-HE code-switching"** is on the Hub as `Hebrish/Jachnun-ONNX` (gated, CC-BY-4.0); recorded under that name, tier Q.
6. `listen-mid` (whisper-small) has no budget in PLAN.md section 3; 256 MiB is proposed in the manifest and marked as such.
